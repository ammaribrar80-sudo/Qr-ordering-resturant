How it works

Three taps from table to kitchen.

A simple guest journey on one side, a clear live order board on the other. No hardware and no app to install.

01
📱

Scan the table code

Every table gets its own QR code. Guests scan it with their camera and the menu opens with the table number already attached.

02
🛒

Pick dishes & quantities

Browse by category, search the menu and build an order. Add a note for the kitchen, then send it in one tap.

03
🔔

Track it live

The kitchen sees the order instantly with its table number. Guests follow the status from received to served, right on their phone.

Built for service

Everything the floor and kitchen need.

A focused ordering flow for guests and a no-nonsense dashboard for staff. Menu changes, table counts and QR codes are all managed in one place.

  • 📋

    Live order board

    New, preparing, ready and served — with sound alerts and auto-refresh.

  • 🔢

    Correct table, every time

    Each QR encodes its table, so orders arrive at the right place automatically.

  • ✏️

    Menu you control

    Add dishes, change prices and mark items sold out from the dashboard.

  • 🖨️

    Print-ready QR sheet

    Generate a tidy sheet of table codes and print it in a couple of clicks.
